📘 Assessment & Grading Engine Operating Guide

System Rules & Logic
1. 📊 Term 1 vs. Term 2 Consistency

When you configure Term 1, the system prompts you to complete Term 2. Before confirming Term 2, the system automatically compares both terms and notifies you if they match or differ.

2. 🌍 Multi-Year Global Policy Inheritance

You do not need to re-create assessment policies every single year. The active format set by the Headmaster applies automatically to all current and future academic years until modified.

3. 📜 Historical Transcript Lock Protection

Updating an assessment policy for a new term will never alter or corrupt historical student transcripts or past year results. Previously recorded student grades remain 100% frozen and preserved.
