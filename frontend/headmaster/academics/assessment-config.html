🏛️ National Curriculum Profiles & Multi-Year Milestone Pipelines

The background system automatically harvests historical student performance across multi-year cycles for official NECTA CAMS uploads while maintaining standalone 100% internal school reports.

🎒 Primary Profile (Standard 1–7)
• Internal Reports: Standalone Term 1 (100%) & Term 2 (100%) for all classes.
• National Milestones: Standard 4 (SFNA) and Standard 7 (PSLE) national exam tracking.
🏫 O-Level Secondary Profile (Form 1–4)
• Internal Reports: Routine term marks captured out of 100% for all grades (Form 1–4).
• NECTA CAMS Harvester: Form 1 marks are strictly excluded. Multi-year harvester compiles Form 2 FTNA (20%), Form 3 Annual (30%), Form 4 Mock (40%), and Project Portfolio (10%).
🎓 A-Level Secondary Profile (Form 5–6)
• Internal Reports: Routine term marks captured out of 100% (Form 5 & 6).
• NECTA CAMS Harvester: Aggregates Form 5 Term 1 (10%), Form 5 Annual (20%), Form 6 Term 1 (20%), Form 6 Mock (40%), and Project Portfolio (10%).
🛡️ Weights are strictly enforced across Marks Entry, Broadsheets, Transcripts, and NECTA CAMS Harvesters.
